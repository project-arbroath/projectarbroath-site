Our Civic Mission

What is the point of Scotland being in the union?
Is there a point?

We know why Scotland's in the union—the UK. We were forced into it. Or tricked, if you prefer. One thing's for sure—we didn't march into it on 1 May 1707 singing and dancing in a big parade.

So if there is a point to Scotland being in the union, then it had better be a good one.

The union affects Scotland every day, in every way, in every part of our lives. In our electricity bills. In our fishing waters. In decisions made by governments we didn't elect, about weapons we didn't ask for, stored in waters we live beside.

If the union is affecting our lives and our futures every day, shouldn't we ask: what is the point of Scotland being in the union?

PROTOTYPE ANALYSIS

Socratic Card Spinner

UX Solution: This "spinner" holds 4 separate modules within a single desktop asset. Rather than overwhelming a visitor with static rows of content, it layers them into an elegant, timed interaction.

Active Panel Detail:

Select a card or wait for autoplay to view its specific conversational rationale.

The Dynamic Feel:

On hover, the carousel pauses so a user can read the text completely. The progress lines at the bottom act as indicators to reveal how many panels exist and invite click-throughs.

Active Viewport Rule:

On Wide Desktop screens, this spinner acts as a dynamic sidebar. On mobile, it vanishes intentionally so your core explainer text enjoys 100% of the reading layout above the fold.

💡 Visualizer Tip: Hover your cursor over the card to pause the auto-scroller. Toggle viewports to test device behavior.
